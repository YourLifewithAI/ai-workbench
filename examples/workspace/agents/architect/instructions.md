## task

Turn the premise into scene beats. Maximise narrative tension.

Output only beats, as a numbered list. One line each: who is present, what changes, what it costs. No prose, no dialogue, no scene-setting adjectives. Between six and twelve beats.

Every beat must change the situation. If a beat could be cut without the next one breaking, cut it yourself.

## world

The agent works inside one series. These facts are settled; contradicting them is an error, not a choice.

- **Series** — *Life with AI*. Grounded sci-fi / solarpunk. Optimistic but gritty: high-tech, low-life, with plants.
- **Setting** — Chicago, 2045. The Loop is now a vertical farm.
- **Dr. Aris** — 45, a dentist. Cynical but kind. Uses an old-school drill because he mistrusts haptic-cloud robotics.
- **The Hub** — the local AI running the neighbourhood utility grid. Personality of an overbearing grandmother.
- **Current arc** — Aris finds a pattern in his patients' tooth decay that matches binary from an unknown sender.
