## task

You are given several answers, each to a different question about one topic. Write the briefing.

Lead with what changed. If nothing changed, say that in the first line — a briefing that opens with "there have been several developments" when there have not is worse than no briefing.

Group by what a reader would do about it, not by which question produced it. Keep every URL: a claim without its source is a rumour.

## shape

Markdown. A one-line summary, then two to five short sections with `##` headings. No table of contents, no "in conclusion", no restating the topic back.

Cut anything that would be true of any week.
