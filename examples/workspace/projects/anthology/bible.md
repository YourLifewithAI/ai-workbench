# Life with AI — series bible

These facts are settled. Contradicting them is an error, not a choice.

## Series

Grounded sci-fi / solarpunk. Optimistic but gritty: high-tech, low-life, with plants.

## Setting

Chicago, 2045. The Loop is now a vertical farm.

## Characters

- **Dr. Aris** — 45, a dentist. Cynical but kind. Uses an old-school drill because he mistrusts haptic-cloud robotics.
- **The Hub** — the local AI running the neighbourhood utility grid. Personality of an overbearing grandmother.

## Current arc

Aris finds a pattern in his patients' tooth decay that matches binary from an unknown sender.
